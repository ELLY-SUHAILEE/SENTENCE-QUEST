Choose Your Grammar Mission

Each mission will help you build better English sentences.

👦 📚
MISSION 01

Nouns & Pronouns

Learn how to name people, animals, places and things and replace nouns with pronouns.

Sarah is reading. → She is reading.
🦒 🐘
MISSION 02

Adjectives

Use describing words to make sentences more interesting.

The giraffe is tall.
🐱 🪑
MISSION 03

Prepositions

Describe where people, animals and objects are.

The cat is under the chair.
🕘 ⏰
MISSION 04

Time

Learn to talk about daily activities and time.

I go to school at seven o'clock.
⚽ 🏃
MISSION 05

Present Simple

Talk about routines, habits and things we usually do.

Adam plays football every Sunday.
👧 📖
MISSION 06

Present Continuous

Describe actions that are happening now.

The girl is reading a book.
🐦 ✈️
MISSION 07

Modals

Use words such as can and can't to talk about abilities.

A bird can fly.
🏖️ 📸
MISSION 08

Past Tense

Build sentences about things that happened in the past.

We visited the beach yesterday.
🍎 🍌
MISSION 09

Determiners & Quantifiers

Use words such as a, an, some and many correctly in sentences.

There are some apples.

🏆 YEAR 3 FINAL MISSION

After completing your grammar missions, look at a picture and write a complete English sentence by yourself.

👧 + 📖 + 📚

The girl is reading a book in the library.
